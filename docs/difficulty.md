# Difficulty: how the campaign is measured

Generated by `bun scripts/audit-levels.ts` from `src/data/levels.json` (built by `bun scripts/build-levels.ts`).
Do not edit by hand.

## The players

- **Natural starts** (`src/core/players.ts`) play the voyage port by port through `simulate()`, as a person
  does with the live projection, and skip a box when the projection turns red: greedy by freight, greedy by
  freight per tonne, fill-the-tank-first (buy a full tank at the first pump), and a myopic player that picks
  each call's load against that call's stem only (multi-port levels). Bunkers: the Chief's cheapest safe plan on
  AUTO levels (World 1), otherwise NEED at every pump (enough to reach the next pump on the reserve — the
  "gas station" habit, the trap at dry ports and price spreads). Boxes go on the stack that keeps the most
  later boxes placeable.
- **Sensible human** (playtest 30): the greedy-by-density and greedy-by-freight loads improved by single changes
  (add a box, land one, swap one for one) until no single change pays more, with the better of NEED and the
  cheapest bunkers. ★ must beat it on every generated level; the authored tutorials (whose lesson is that one
  change) only ask ★ to beat the instinctive plans.
- **Strong editor**: 64 games, each from a natural start (density 40%, freight 30%, tank 15%, myopic 15%; half
  of them with ±18% jitter on the preferences), best improvement on the manifest landscape (exact values:
  freight − the cheapest bunker plan, stowage checked) over add / drop / 1↔1 / 2↔1 / 1↔2, 5% slips, at most 20
  edits. On levels with a manual valve (World 2 on) half of the runs value a load with NEED bunkers instead of the
  cheapest plan (critic 21's gas-station fuel): they never think of carrying cheap fuel or stemming past a dry port
  beyond what NEED asks. A strong human's first session.
- **Careful editor**: 3-exchange best improvement from every natural start and 6 random maximal loads, no slips.
  The fairness check.
- **Random tinkerer** (critic 20's gate): 40 random legal edits on the plan from an empty ship (add a box on a
  random legal stack, land one, move one, bunkers ± a lot, NEED), keeping every edit that does not lower the
  score (an unsafe plan scores below every safe one). 200 runs.

## The numbers

- **V\*** — the exact optimum (`src/core/solver.ts`), = ★★★. **ω** — optimal manifests, identical boxes merged.
- **Stars** — ★ = the best natural start (instinct and the sensible human) + 1, so neither passes from L2 on;
  ★★ = the best decoy + 1 if that is above ★, else the midpoint; ★★★ = V\*. Each at least 2 above the one below where
  the range allows, and ★★ always above ★ when the range is 2 or more; only lesson levels (T0, B1, B2) may ship a
  narrower range, where ★★ meets ★★★ (or all three coincide).
- **Greedy** — the best natural start; **gap** = 1 − greedy / V\*.
- **X** — exchange depth: the smallest k whose k-exchange climb (k boxes out and k in per edit) from at least half
  of the natural starts reaches V\* (4 = none up to 3).
- **Decoys** — 1-exchange local optima worth ≥ 90% of V\* (every single add, drop or swap makes it worse).
- **S★ / S★★★** — share of strong runs reaching ★ / ★★★; **C★★★** — share of careful runs reaching ★★★;
  **T★ / T★★★** — share of tinkerer runs reaching ★ / ★★★ (gate on B3+: ≤ 50% / ≤ 10%).
- **DI** = 40(1 − S★) + 30(1 − S★★★) + 30(1 − C★★★): 0 = trivial, 100 = nobody wins.
- **Mechanics** — wt fuel weighs, bd bands, ca calls, pu pickups, dry no-pump port, st stacks (never bury), bar
  harbour bar, $ price spread, lot barge lots, hv heavy below light, rf reefers, ct contract, zn winter zone,
  br bridge. **Bold** = introduced here; ° = the ablation found no change in V\* (the rule is present but idle).

Bands (docs/design-process/13 §2.3): B1 breather (exact fit), B2 intro, B3 normal, B4 normal-high, B5 spike, B6 boss;
targets slide from area 1 to area 8 (`src/core/targets.ts`).
